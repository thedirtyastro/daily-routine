03 · PROGRESSION

How to keep improving

Rep range

When you reach the top of the rep range with clean form on every set, add a small amount of backpack weight or use a harder variation.

1–3 reps in reserve

Keep about 1–3 good repetitions in reserve. Do not sacrifice technique to chase numbers.

Push-up path

Regular → harder variation → backpack push-up.

Squat path

Bodyweight → slow squat → backpack squat → split squat.

Shoulder path

Light bottle/backpack → more repetitions → slightly more resistance.

Back path

Add books or water bottles to the backpack gradually.

06:30 AM

Keep the wake time around 6:30 AM.

06 · HAIR GROWTH + CARE

Simple hair routine

Wash

Wash according to how oily/dirty your scalp gets. Apply shampoo mainly to the scalp rather than aggressively scrubbing hair lengths.

Condition

Use conditioner after washing.

Gentle handling

Handle wet hair gently. Avoid aggressive towel rubbing and tugging.

Heat

Limit excessive heat from dryers/straighteners.

Tension

Avoid hairstyles that repeatedly pull tightly on the hair.

Supplements

Avoid random high-dose hair supplements. Too much of some nutrients can worsen hair loss.

Nutrition

Avoid crash dieting and inadequate calorie/protein intake.

When to get checked

If thinning, bald patches, receding hairline or heavy shedding persists, see a dermatologist to identify the cause.

Simple daily care: Morning gentle styling only; after sweating, wash/rinse as appropriate for your scalp; wash days use gentle shampoo + conditioner; after washing gently squeeze/wrap hair dry; at night keep hair loose and avoid tight styles.

12 · SAFETY

Terrace + training safety

  • Exercise well away from the terrace edge.
  • Use only stable surfaces for support.
  • Do not use an unstable chair/table for exercises.
  • Warm up before hard sets.
  • Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, faintness or unusual breathing difficulty.
  • If you have persistent or significant hair loss, seek medical evaluation rather than relying only on home remedies.
13 · HAIR CARE NOTE

Evidence note

The hair-care section follows the guidance included in the source guide: wash based on scalp needs, use conditioner, handle hair gently, limit excessive heat, avoid damaging tension, maintain adequate nutrition, and seek dermatologic evaluation when hair loss is concerning.
